Puzzlet Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Puzzlet is a collection of offline puzzle games for Android, published on Google Play by Rakesh Kumawat. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing.

Data we collect

None. Puzzlet does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no network access. The app does not request the Internet permission.

Data stored on your device

Puzzlet keeps your game progress, best scores, achievements, daily challenge streak and settings in the app's private storage on your device so they are there the next time you play. This data never leaves your device through the app. You can erase it at any time with "Reset progress" in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Android backup

Puzzlet opts out of Android's automatic app-data backup, so your progress is not copied to your Google account backup. Because of this, progress does not move to a new phone; it stays on the device where you played.

Permissions

Puzzlet requests no runtime permissions. It uses the device's vibration motor for optional haptic feedback, which needs no permission, and plays short built-in sounds.

Children

Puzzlet is not directed at children under 13. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at the same address with an updated effective date.
